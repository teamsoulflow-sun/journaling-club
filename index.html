Questions

Before you open the first page…

What if I have never journaled consistently?

Perfect. The club is specifically structured for people who struggle with consistency. The goal is not a perfect streak. It is learning how to return.

What if I join after the club has already started?

This is a rolling program. Your Day 1 starts when you join, while you participate in the club's current weekly theme. You do not need to complete a backlog.

What if I miss a few days?

Come back to today's page. No catching up required.

Do I have to share my private journal?

No. Your actual journal stays private. Accountability is about showing up, not showing your pages.

What happens on Tuesdays?

Tuesday at 9 PM is the Live Journal Lab, where you learn a deeper journaling method and receive the week's special journal practice.
