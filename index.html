Questions

Before you open a fresh page…

What if I've never journaled consistently before?

That's exactly what the club is built for. The daily practice is intentionally small, and the live classes teach you different ways to journal so you don't get stuck staring at a blank page.

How much time do I need every day?

Most daily prompts are designed for about 5–10 minutes. You can always stay longer when a page wants more of you.

What happens if I join late?

You start with today's prompt. Your personal Day 1 begins when you join, while you participate in the club's current theme. Past prompts can be explored later without becoming homework.

Do I have to share what I write?

No. Your journal can remain completely private. Accountability is about showing up, not exposing personal writing.

What happens in the Tuesday live classes?

Tuesday's Journal Lab introduces a practical journaling style or application and gives you the week's deeper journal exercise.

Are replays available?

Weekly class replays are added to the member space so you can revisit the learning when needed.
